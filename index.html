The Artist

Hi, I'm Barbora.

With over 10 years of experience, I see the skin not just as a surface, but as a living canvas waiting for a story to be told. I specialize primarily in fine-line, blackwork, and dotwork.

In my studio, I prioritize absolute sterility, safety, and a welcoming atmosphere. Every piece I create is a collaboration—a blend of your vision and my artistic expression.

"I don't copy other people's work. Every piece that comes from my hands is an original created specifically for you."
